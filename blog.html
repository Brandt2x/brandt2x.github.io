How I Passed the CompTIA Security+

Posted April 2026

Passing the Security+ was a big milestone in my transition into cybersecurity. I focused on consistency, hands on practice, and understanding the “why” behind concepts instead of just memorizing terms.

Read more

1. I used the exam objectives as my roadmap.

I treated each objective like a checklist and made sure I could explain each one in simple terms.

2. I practiced questions regularly.

I used practice questions to find weak spots, then reviewed those topics until I felt comfortable.

3. Hands on work helped a lot.

My home lab made topics like monitoring, logs, and security operations feel real instead of theoretical.

4. I stayed consistent.

Even on days when I did not study for long, I tried to touch the material and keep momentum going.

My advice:

Focus on understanding concepts, do lots of practice questions, and do not underestimate how much hands on work helps.
